## What it will do

A gym training app. Planned: ready-made programmes or your own plans, logging sets, reps and weight, a rest timer, records and progress charts, and an exercise library. It should guide a beginner with sensible defaults and give an advanced lifter full control.

## Status

The project is in progress. The app foundation, the approved look and design system, a local database with migrations and the logic are done: estimated one-rep max (1RM), volume, records and a weight suggestion for the next session, with tests. The workout and progress screens come next.

## Technology

Expo, React Native and TypeScript in strict mode. Data stays on the phone in SQLite, with no account and no server. Jest tests, linting and checks in GitHub Actions.
