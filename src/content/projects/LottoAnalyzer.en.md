## What it does

An app for analysing Polish Lotto results. An educational, just-for-fun project: no analysis improves the odds of winning, because every combination of numbers is equally likely.

- **Dashboard** — summary statistics, latest results, “hot” and “cold” numbers, a frequency chart.
- **Statistics** — frequency of every number, filtered by period (week, month, year).
- **Generator** — number-picking “strategies” (random, hot, cold, balanced), just for fun.
- **History** — browsing past draws.
- **Updates and import** — fetching the latest results from lotto.pl and importing data from CSV.

## Technology

Electron and JavaScript, with data in a local SQLite database created on first launch.
